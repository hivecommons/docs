# Join Hive Commons

Hive Commons is an umbrella for **like-minded open source projects** that make
working with AI coding agents safer, more observable, and more accountable.
If that describes your project, we invite you to propose joining the family.

## What we look for

- **Removes real friction** for people working with coding agents:
  orchestration, governance, spec-driven workflows, attribution, session
  tooling, observability, safety.
- **Shares our values.** Openness (design and decisions happen in public),
  human accountability (every action traces to a named person), and governance
  enforced in code rather than in prose.
- **Open source, properly.** Apache 2.0 (or willing to relicense), DCO
  sign-off on every commit, CNCF Code of Conduct.
- **Maintained.** At least one active maintainer willing to show up at the
  biweekly community meeting while the project settles in.

Projects do not need to depend on Hive. hotshot, pluk, promptargs and dibs are
all useful entirely on their own — they belong here because they solve
adjacent problems for the same people.

## What you get

- A home in the [hivecommons](https://github.com/hivecommons) GitHub
  organization with shared governance, security policy, and CI conventions.
- A section on this docs site, synced from your repository, and a card on
  [hivecommons.dev](https://hivecommons.dev).
- A slot in the community meeting and the mailing lists to reach Hive
  operators and contributors.
- Optionally, a hive of your own: Hive can run governed agent swarms against
  your repositories as a spoke of the Hive Commons hub.

## How to propose a project

Open a **project proposal** issue — it is a short form:

**[→ Propose a project to join Hive Commons](https://github.com/hivecommons/.github/issues/new?template=join-hive-commons.yml)**

The form asks for the repository, what the project does, how it fits the
family, license, maintainers, and adoption. Maintainers decide by lazy
consensus per [GOVERNANCE.md](https://github.com/hivecommons/.github/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md)
and will reply on the issue; if it's a fit we'll invite you to present at a
community meeting and work through the move together.

## Other ways to get involved

Not ready to bring a whole project? You're still welcome:

- **Run a hive** against your own repositories — see
  [Getting started with Hive](/docs/hive/getting-started) and add yourself to
  [ADOPTERS.md](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v5/ADOPTERS.md).
- **Contribute** to any repository in the organization —
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/hivecommons/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  covers the contributor ladder.
- **Join the conversation** on
  [hivecommons-dev@googlegroups.com](https://hivecommons.dev/join),
  [Discord](https://hivecommons.dev/discord), or the biweekly
  [community meeting](/docs/community/meetings).
